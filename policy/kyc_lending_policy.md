# FinGuard Synthetic KYC & Lending Policy

(Synthetic policy used as the Bedrock Knowledge Base corpus. Not real bank policy.)

## KYC-1 — PAN format
PAN must be 10 characters: five letters, four digits, one letter. Reject unreadable or malformed PAN numbers and request a clear copy.

## KYC-2 — Blacklist
Applications presenting a PAN on the fraud blacklist must be escalated to the fraud team immediately; do not inform the applicant.

## KYC-3 — ID validity
Photo ID must be valid on the application date. Expired identity documents are not acceptable; request a current ID.

## KYC-4 — Customer record match
Name, date of birth and PAN on the application must match the existing customer record. A PAN registered to a different customer indicates possible forgery and must be escalated.

## LND-1 — Income eligibility
Minimum verified monthly income is INR 25,000 for any loan application.

## LND-2 — Loan-to-income cap
Requested loan amount must not exceed five times verified annual income.

## LND-3 — Large loans
Loans above INR 50,00,000 require senior underwriter review regardless of automated checks.

## LND-4 — Income consistency
Stated income more than 50 percent above the income on record must be supported by fresh salary proof before processing.
